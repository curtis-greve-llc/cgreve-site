# I've Been Named to the ASD Market Week Reverse Logistics Advisory Board

**June 2026 · Advisory · Reverse Logistics**

I was recently invited to join the **Reverse Logistics Advisory Board** for [ASD Market Week](https://asdonline.com/) — and I accepted without hesitation. Here's why this matters, and what I'm bringing to the table.

## What ASD Market Week Is

[ASD Market Week](https://asdonline.com/about/show-overview/) is the largest wholesale retail merchandise trade show in the United States. Held twice a year at the Las Vegas Convention Center, it connects more than 30,000 attendees — retailers, distributors, importers, and e-commerce operators — with 1,800+ vendors across 19+ product categories.

It's not a niche show. It's where serious wholesale commerce gets done.

And increasingly, the secondary market — liquidation, closeouts, customer returns, and overstock — is front and center at ASD. Recommerce isn't a fringe category anymore. It's a strategic channel for retailers and buyers alike, and ASD recognizes that.

## Why Reverse Logistics Belongs at This Table

The numbers are unambiguous. The U.S. reverse logistics market hit approximately $936 billion in 2026 and is projected to approach $1.75 trillion by 2035. Returns, excess inventory, and secondary market redistribution are no longer the tail end of the supply chain — they are core supply chain infrastructure.

The buyers and sellers who come to ASD are operating in that market every day. They're sourcing closeouts, bidding on return loads, evaluating manifests, and making buy/sell decisions that depend on accurate pricing, channel knowledge, and operational discipline.

What they need — and what this advisory board is positioned to provide — is better industry guidance, better education, and better frameworks for navigating that complexity.

That's work I've been doing for 40 years.

## What I'm Bringing

I've spent my career inside the largest liquidation and reverse logistics operations in the country:

* **Head of Walmart's Reverse Logistics Division** — operations and reverse logistics at scale
* **President of GENCO** — one of the largest 3PL and reverse logistics companies in North America
* **VP of Liquidation & Remarketing at Inmar Intelligence** — leading liquidation strategy, remarketing, and business development

I'm also a co-founder of [Liquidata.ai](https://liquidata.ai), an AI-powered manifest analysis platform built specifically for wholesale liquidation buyers — the same buyers who show up at ASD looking for an edge.

Through [Curtis Greve LLC](https://cgreve.com), I work directly with retailers, manufacturers, and 3PLs to turn their reverse logistics programs from cost centers into profit centers.

I've been on both sides of the table: the seller trying to maximize recovery, and the buyer trying to price a load accurately without getting burned. That perspective is exactly what this advisory role calls for.

## What This Means for the ASD Community

My goal on this board is straightforward: help ASD serve the reverse logistics and liquidation community better. That means:

* Elevating the quality of education and programming around reverse logistics
* Connecting buyers and sellers with real operational knowledge, not just theory
* Advocating for tools and practices that make the secondary market more transparent and more profitable for everyone who participates honestly in it

If you're in the liquidation or reverse logistics space and plan to be at ASD — [let's connect](https://cgreve.com/#contact). I'd welcome the conversation.
